    House rules

    1. Written in ink

    Each play is published the evening before with its entry, stop loss, take profit and maximum hold. Nothing is changed after it is published.

    2. No hindsight

    Entry is the next opening price. If a stop and a target are both touched in one day, the stop counts. A gap past a level fills at the open. A play that opens beyond its levels is void.

    3. Big names only

    Large, heavily traded US stocks and ETFs, or their listed options. No penny stocks, no small coins, no promotions, no paid picks. Options are bought, never sold.
