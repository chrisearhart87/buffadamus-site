    Written in ink

    Each play is published after the close with its entry, stop loss, take profit and a five-session limit. Entry is the next opening price. A gap through a level fills at the open, and if both levels are touched in one day the stop counts. Nothing is edited after publishing.

    The treasury guard

    • No play risks more than 1% of the bank at its stop, and no stock play is bigger than 25% of the bank.
    • Options are bought only, at most 2.5% of the bank each and two a week.
    • At most 4 plays open, 4% of the bank at risk, two per sector.
    • A 2% losing day sits out the next session. A 5% slide in five sessions pauses new plays until Monday. A 15% loss stops the desk until a human resets it.

    Big names only

    Large, heavily traded US stocks and funds, or their listed options. No penny stocks, coins, promotions or paid picks.
